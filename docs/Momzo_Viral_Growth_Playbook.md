# Momzo — Viral Growth Playbook (v2: community-first)

**Strategy:** Build a mom **community** with a content brand first → then launch the app to a warm, waiting audience.
**Channels:** Instagram (Reels + carousels) + Facebook (Page + groups). No TikTok, no paid influencers.
**Content:** Child psychology & development, radically simplified — tiny text, one insight, one activity.
**Launch hero:** **"Know Your Child"** — the expert AI asks a few questions about your kid, reads the behaviour, and tells you what to do.
**North star:** Phase A → an engaged, growing mom audience + a waitlist. Phase B → activated moms who invite another mom.

# PHASE A — BUILD THE COMMUNITY

## 1. Platform roles: Instagram + Facebook

- **Instagram = your growth engine.**
  - **Reels** = reach (new moms find you). AI-generated (§3).
  - **Carousels** = saves + authority (moms save "what to do" posts and come back). Your existing pipeline already nails these.
  - **Broadcast Channel** = your owned audience + waitlist warmer (moms opt in, you message them free — this becomes your launch list).
- **Facebook = distribution + community depth.**
  - **Page** = repost your IG content (moms 35+ live here; free reach).
  - **Groups (other people's)** = seeding. Answer questions, drop value, never spam. This is where you find your first few hundred moms.
  - **Your own FB group (optional)** = only if you want true community interaction and can moderate it. Otherwise skip it and use the IG Broadcast Channel + waitlist as your "community" — lighter, and it doesn't recreate the social-media overwhelm Momzo exists to escape.

> Recommendation: **owned audience > group** for launch. Prioritize followers + a waitlist you can message directly. A FB group is a "nice to have," not the priority.

## 2. The content formula (your repeatable unit)

Every post — carousel or Reel — is the same 5-beat structure. This consistency is what trains the algorithm *and* builds the brand:

```
1. HOOK (1 line, recognition)   "Why your 7-year-old says 'no' to literally everything"
2. THE INSIGHT (2–3 tiny lines) simplified child psych — the real reason
3. THE REFRAME                  "It's not defiance — his brain is testing that he's safe to."
4. THE ACTIVITY (the payoff)    "Try tonight: give him 2 choices you're happy with either way."
5. CTA                          "Save this 💛 + tag a mom whose kid does this too."
   + soft Momzo watermark
```

Rules that make it work:
- **Tiny text.** One idea per post. If a mom can't get it in 3 seconds, cut it.
- **Always end on an activity.** The activity is your differentiator *and* your save-bait — "learn → do" is exactly your app's promise, so the content *is* a free sample of the product.
- **The activity is the shareable payoff** (this is your "scan result"): a mom screenshots "try this tonight" and sends it to her mom group. Design the last card to look good alone.

**Content pillars (rotate these):** big emotions/meltdowns · "what to say instead of…" · why-your-kid-does-X (behaviour explainers) · 5-minute connection activities · gentle-parenting green flags · quick wins for busy moms.

## 3. The AI content factory (no budget, no creators)

You don't need influencers — you become a **faceless brand engine** (her cheap-reach tactic, but it's *your* brand, which is better). Build on the pipeline you already run:

**Carousels (already working):**
NotebookLM (idea) → Claude (script + image prompts) → Nano Banana (images) → Canva template → caption → post to IG + FB. Keep this; just feed it the §2 formula and the §4 testing sheet.

**AI Reels (new) — three cheap formats, no face needed:**
1. **Comic-style Reel** (you're already moving here) — 3–5 AI comic frames of a relatable mom-and-kid moment, animated in Canva/CapCut, trending audio, text overlay = the §2 formula. Highest brand value.
2. **Text-over-broll** — AI voiceover or silent text over calm stock footage (kid drawing, family dinner). Fastest to mass-produce; ideal for volume.
3. **Animated carousel-to-video** — turn a winning carousel into a swipe-timed Reel. Free reuse of proven content.

**Volume plan:** 1 Reel/day + 3–4 carousels/week to start. Reels for reach, carousels for saves. Warm the account 5–7 days with pure value before any app teasing.

## 4. The content variable-testing system (her crown jewel — kept)

Turn "going viral" from luck into a machine. Log every post in a sheet, change **one variable at a time**, kill losers, clone winners:

| Variable | Test |
|---|---|
| Hook wording | "Why your kid says no" vs "The real reason your 7yo melts down" vs "POV: bedtime is a battle" |
| Format | comic Reel vs text-over-broll vs carousel |
| Emotional register | guilt-relief vs "aha/educational" vs wholesome |
| The payoff | activity vs brain-science stat vs reframe line |
| Caption tag-bait | "tag a mom who needs this" vs "send to your mom group" |
| Hashtags | #gentleparenting #momsofinstagram #parentingtips vs niche/local |
| Audio | trending sound vs voiceover |
| Post time | **6–8am** · **~1pm** · **9–10pm** (after bedtime = the big one) |
| CTA | "save this" vs "comment WORD for the free guide" (§5 lead magnet) |

Within ~2 weeks one hook/format will do most of your reach (your Pareto 20%). Everything then replicates it.

## 5. Community & waitlist mechanics (turn viewers into launch users)

Followers aren't enough — you need moms you can *message on launch day*. Three loops:

1. **Tag loop (free targeted reach).** Every post earns a tag: "@her this is us." A tag = a free impression to a perfectly-matched new mom. This is moms' version of Glam Up's comment-codes.
2. **Lead-magnet → waitlist (the bridge to launch).** On your best carousels: *"Comment CALM and I'll DM you the free '5 scripts for meltdowns' guide."* The comment boosts reach; the DM captures her. Inside the DM: the free guide **+** "want early access to Momzo when it opens? join the founding-moms list 💛" → link to waitlist / IG Broadcast Channel. This converts anonymous reach into an owned, warm launch list.
3. **Founding Moms (your beta = your seed).** Invite 30–50 waitlist moms to early access. They give you real testimonials, real App Store reviews, and become your first referrers. This *replaces* Glam Up's fake reviews with the genuine article.

**Phase A is "done" when** you've got a few thousand engaged followers and a waitlist of a few hundred founding moms — not a fixed date, a threshold. Then you launch into warm demand.

# PHASE B — LAUNCH THE APP

## 6. The "Know Your Child" hero launch

This is your "scan" — the curiosity hook that gets moms in the door. The AI asks a few questions about her kid, interprets the behaviour, and gives tailored tips. Lead the entire launch with it.

- **Pre-launch teasing (Phase A):** seed curiosity for weeks — *"What if you actually understood WHY your kid does that? We built something…"* Build demand before the door opens.
- **The free hook:** "Know Your Child" is free to try — it's the value moment that earns the download (like Glam Up's free scan), then the rest of the app + Momzo+ follow.
- **The shareable insight card** = your viral artifact. After the AI reads her child, generate a warm, screenshot-worthy summary card ("Aarav is a sensitive, deeply-feeling kid who needs the 'why' before the 'no' 💛") with a soft watermark + link. Moms post these to stories → other moms ask "what app is this?" → referral loop fires.
  - **Keep it honest and safe:** it's *insight and tips grounded in real child psychology*, not a diagnosis or a personality-type label. That framing is both more trustworthy and keeps you clear of pseudo-clinical claims.

## 7. The referral engine (value-unlock — you liked this)

- **Invite 2 mom friends → you both get Momzo+ free for a month.** Double-sided. Never gate the *core* value behind referrals (that feels manipulative to moms) — gate the *premium* upgrade.
- **WhatsApp is the detonator.** One-tap share with a pre-written warm message + her link. Moms forward to their school-class / mom groups — that's where this spreads, not stranger comments.
- **Shareable cards carry the loop:** the Know-Your-Child card, the quiz result, the weekly recap — each watermarked + linked. The child's face is never required to share.

## 8. The onboarding funnel (friction removal + emotional timing — kept)

- **No-friction start** — let her feel one real insight before asking for signup.
- **Child profile = the investment moment** (your version of Glam Up's selfie upload) — once she's told you about *her* kid, she's in. Place it before any upgrade ask.
- **Real reveal** — the actual Know-Your-Child insight + a useful "right now" script. Real beats fake.
- **Delay the paywall** to a **win-moment** — right after an insight lands or a script works. *How and when you ask matters more than the price* (her biggest lesson).
- **Honest rating prompt** fired after that same win (not a forced gate).
- **Honest win-back** — dismiss upgrade → "first month 50% off" once. (Her decline→discount, cleaned up.)

## 9. Monetization timing (brief)
Free core (Know Your Child + daily card + basic activities) → habit → Momzo+ (unlimited AI, full library, quiz, WhatsApp reminders, memory timeline), asked at an emotional peak. You're building for retention (per your PRD), so *actually keep them* — that's the opposite of Glam Up's cash-out model, and it's your advantage.

# CROSS-CUTTING

## 10. The sprint

**Phase A — Weeks 1–4+ (build the community)**
- Wk 1: warm 1–2 IG accounts + FB Page; publish the §2 formula daily (Reels) + 3–4 carousels; start the testing sheet; seed 5–10 FB groups value-first.
- Wk 2: find your winning hook/format; turn on the lead-magnet → waitlist loop.
- Wk 3–4: scale the winner; grow the waitlist; recruit Founding Moms; tease "Know Your Child."
- Exit when: few-thousand engaged followers + few-hundred waitlist.

**Phase B — Launch week onward**
- Open Know Your Child to the waitlist first (Founding Moms) → collect real reviews.
- Turn on referral value-unlock + WhatsApp share + shareable insight cards.
- Time Momzo+ prompts to win-moments; honest win-back on.
- Watch retention, not just installs.

## 11. Mindset (the parts that are right)
Design for shareability, not features · don't marry a channel until it proves itself · systematize the winner · big swings > button tweaks · test 10 things a week · delusional faith + fast iteration.

## 12. Metrics

**Phase A (community):** follower growth · save rate (carousels) · share/tag rate (Reels) · waitlist signups · lead-magnet conversion.
**Phase B (app):** onboarding completion ≥70% · Day-2 activation ≥40% · Week-4 retention ≥25% (your real moat) · referral share rate (north star) · K-factor → toward 1 · cost per install ≈ ₹0.
