# How to Make a Family Media Plan

Media is everywhere and managing it all can be tough. Having a plan can help.  
Create rules for media and technology that work for your family.

Every family is different, so create a plan that fits your routines and values. Discuss them with your kids.

---

## Example Rules to Consider

- **Set screen-free zones** (such as the dinner table, during homework, and before bed) to strengthen family connections, promote learning, and improve sleep.  
- **One screen at a time rule**: Turn off devices not in use to reduce distractions.  
- **Turn off autoplay and notifications**: These features are designed to keep kids engaged longer.  
- **Choose quality content**: Use apps and shows that teach, inspire creativity, and encourage social connection. Avoid content with excessive ads, violence, or age-inappropriate material.  
- **Check privacy settings**: Talk about ads and influencer marketing before using new apps or platforms so kids learn what's real and what's designed to sell.  
- **Keep room for other activities**: Add swaps like reading, outdoor play, family games, and hobbies so screens don’t crowd out other experiences.  
- **Use parental controls**: Track family digital habits and set controls (time, downloads, contacts, purchases) using tools on routers, smartphones, tablets, gaming consoles, social media accounts, and school devices.  

---

## Family-Wide Planning

- Create a plan for **every member of your family**, including yourself.  
- Remember: You are an important role model in practicing healthy digital habits and establishing a healthy relationship with technology.  

---

## Revisiting the Plan

Revisit and revise your plan often.  
- The beginning of each school year or during summer and holiday breaks are great times to review.  
- Plans should evolve as your child matures and develops.
